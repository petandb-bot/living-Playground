HOW TO PLAY

PLAY starts a sparse 16-step groove. Tap a sound chip to play immediately. Drag a chip by its colored nameplate to move it.

LINK, then choose source and destination. TICK sends musical events; THUMP and CHIRP can affect each other's next sound.

BUG walks between linked chips. Drag and drop the Bug onto a chip to make it knock. Bugs never change the music on their own.

MUTATE / UNDO reshapes a few details without removing the beat. The browser saves your playfield locally. Recording and WAV export are planned for a later milestone.
